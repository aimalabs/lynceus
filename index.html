When to Apply Preprocessing Presets

Telesphorus runs inference directly on the active filtered canvas. Choose the preset tailored to your slide preparation:

  • ⚡ May-Giemsa AI Preset: Use for standard clinical May-Grünwald Giemsa (MGG) peripheral blood smears to normalize varying illumination, circular microscope apertures, and camera sensors across different laboratories.
  • ⚡ Romanowski AI Preset: Use for Wright, Wright-Giemsa, Leishman, or Field stained smears displaying deep purple/azure dye precipitation, overstaining, or false platelet-like debris.
  • Raw RGB (True Color): Use for pre-calibrated digital whole-slide scanners (WSI) that already provide flat-field illumination, white balancing, and rectangular border cropping.
